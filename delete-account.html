Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Crowdora account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 — Delete from the app (instant)

This is the fastest way and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Crowdora app
  2. Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top right
  3. Scroll to Delete My Account
  4. Confirm

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app — for example if your account was suspended or you lost your password — send us a request.

Send an email to destek@crowdora.app from the address registered to your account, including:

  • Your username
  • The subject line: Account deletion request
Send deletion request

We verify that the request comes from the registered email address and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. We will confirm by email once it is done.

What gets deleted

Account and login credentialsDeleted permanently
Username and profileDeleted permanently
All predictions and their resultsDeleted permanently
Prediction reasoning you wroteDeleted permanently
Ratings, streaks and badgesDeleted permanently
Feedback messagesDeleted permanently
Push notification tokenDeleted permanently

What is retained

Aggregated statistics that can no longer be linked to you — such as overall community sentiment for a given day and historical market prices — are retained. This data contains no personal information and cannot be traced back to any individual.

We may also retain limited records where required by law, or where necessary to resolve a dispute or enforce our terms.

This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account permanently removes your prediction history, accuracy rating, streaks and badges. Creating a new account later will start you from zero.
